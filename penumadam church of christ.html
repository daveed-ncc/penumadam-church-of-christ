Who We Are

Penumadam Church of Christ Community

Based in the scenic rural heart of Andhra Pradesh, India, Penumadam Church of Christ stands as both a sacred pillar of Gospel truth and an active refuge of humanitarian aid.

Faith & Bible Teaching

Through sound biblical teaching, our founder conducts deeply enriching Monthly Bible Classes and spiritual guidance every Sunday service to ground families in faith and integrity.

Rescue & Educate Children

We actively reach out to roadside and destitute children—providing warm shelter, nutritious daily meals, fresh clothes, school supplies, and tuition fees to build their bright future.

Honor & Uplift Widows

Abandoned and poverty-stricken widows in our villages receive loving care, monthly food provisions, essential medicines, and emotional comfort so they never feel forgotten.

Our Works of Love

Comprehensive Community Outreach

Faith without works is dead. Here is how our church actively touches lives across Penumadam and neighbouring villages in Andhra Pradesh.

Education for Poor & Roadside Children

We sponsor school enrollment, school uniforms, textbooks, backpacks, and everyday stationery for children who would otherwise be left begging or working on the roadside.

Nutritious Daily Meals

Hunger is a painful reality in poverty. We arrange wholesome, freshly cooked food distributions to guarantee that growing children and aged seniors never go to sleep hungry.

Shelter & Warm Clothes

Providing safe shelter, warm winter blankets, clean clothes, and dignity to vulnerable youngsters and helpless destitute individuals found on streets and station areas.

Widows Protection & Solace

Elderly and destitute widows receive special monthly provision kits with rice, lentils, oil, and healthcare support, ensuring they feel loved, protected, and respected.

Monthly Intensive Bible Class

Conducted regularly every month by Bro. Sanamanda Jacob to deepen theological understanding, build community morals, and equip disciples for gospel work.

Sunday Worship & Services

Weekly gatherings in Penumadam where the congregation worships, breaks bread, shares prayers for healing, and receives uplifting sermons full of hope and gospel grace.
